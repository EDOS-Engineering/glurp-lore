# Glurp-isms

Things Glurp has said, muttered, or written on the back of an invoice.

> "Oi, where'd all me tokens go?"

The classic. Said on discovering a light token pouch.

> "Oi, *there's* where all me tokens went."

The sequel. Said on first opening Ork Meter.

> "Dull and useful is the best kind of magic. Dramatic magic always ends with somebody's tower on fire."

> "It's like lookin' for one wet sock in a river. And payin' for every drop of the river."

On test logs.

> "Open the fridge, close it, open it again. But the fridge doesn't charge me."

On reading the same file five times.

> "Just one more function. Then bed."

Never followed by bed.

> "A horde, improperly conducted, is a very expensive racket."

To cousin Glorp, at dinner, again.

> "Have you *counted*?"

The winning move in most family arguments.

> "I'll believe it when the ball shows it."

> "Read it once, and read it right; trim the flood before the fight."

> "That bug stays. It's part of the charm."

> "Mind the Cold Hearth after lunch."

> "Count every token, make every token count. Then count 'em again, just to be sure. ...No, wait, that's the Forgetful Read."

> "It's not just you. Pull up a stool."

To anyone new in the Discord.

> "Right then. Let's see where it all goes."
